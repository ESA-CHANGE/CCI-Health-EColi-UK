# Climate Change and E. Coli Health Risks in the UK

ESA CHANGE: Climate–Health Adaptation through New Generation Earth observations.  \
[Project website](https://climate.esa.int/es/supporting-the-paris-agreement/CHANGE/)

This repository explores the use of Earth observation parameters for predicting human health water quality issues.

## Description

UK bathing waters are frequently affected by faecal contamination from agricultural runoff, wastewater discharges and storm overflows. These contamination events pose a public-health risk, particularly during the summer bathing season, and may result in beach closures, reduced recreational use and increased exposure to waterborne pathogens. This use case investigates the relationship between environmental conditions, extreme rainfall and contamination by Escherichia coli (E. coli) and intestinal enterococci (IE) in UK bathing waters. It aims to support predictive risk assessment, early warning and improved management of bathing-water quality.
